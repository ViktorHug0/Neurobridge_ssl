## What was tried since sub24 (16-17 Nov) and how it ended

- New model families with soft labels as an extra or swapped member: CBraMod, CBraMod with 4 time patches, EEGPT, LUNA,
  recurrent, TSConv, LaBraM with 4 time patches, ST-EEGFormer from scratch, ST-EEGFormer-base (86 M), ST-EEGFormer with
  coarse patches: none adds. Only ST-EEGFormer-small (pretrained) and, as a swap, pretrained REVE with soft labels do.
  ST-EEGFormer from scratch is clearly negative: its pretraining is what helps.
- Augmentations (time warp, time mask, band drop, image mixup, GCE, Alljoined-only finish, layout targets): neutral or
  negative on two or three folds each. Closed.
- Round 12 "open bank" (using images with no EEG in the loss): closed in every form. Unseen images that can be pushed
  down in a softmax teach the model that the answer is never an unseen image (-72 % alone on the clean gallery).
- Output-side corrections re-tested in the sealed-like regime: nothing (the average output for a new image has a cosine
  of 0.07 with its embedding).
